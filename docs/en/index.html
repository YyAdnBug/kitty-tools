Clipboard History ⌥C

Nothing you copy gets lost

Text (with its formatting), images and files are all kept, and copying something again just moves it to the top. Press ↩ to paste it back into your app, or ⌘1–9 for the Nth item.

  • The selected item expands in place: code gets syntax coloring, colors show their values, links show a title and image
  • Text inside images is searchable too, recognized on your Mac without going online
  • Favorites, snippets and labels; snippets can include placeholders such as the date or where the cursor goes
  • Anything copied from a password manager or that looks like a key is skipped, and you can exclude apps
Everything Clipboard History does
Launcher ⌥Space

Open anything with a few keystrokes

Apps, files, System Settings pages and browser bookmarks are all a search away, and what you use most rises to the top.

  • Calculations and unit conversions show the answer right away; ↩ pastes it
  • “g keyword” searches the web, with popular search sites already set up
  • Lock the screen, empty the Trash, end a process or see what’s using a port in one line, and add your own scripts
  • ⌘K opens the actions menu; keep what you use often in Favorites
Everything the Launcher does
Translate ⌥D

Select and translate, without switching windows

Select text in any app and press the shortcut, and the translation appears. You can also type to translate, translate whatever you copy, or select part of the screen to translate a screenshot.

  • The built-in service works out of the box, and more than a dozen translation services and language models can join in, side by side
  • Language model translations appear as they’re written
  • Look up a single word for a dictionary entry, or replace the original text with the translation
  • Translation history and a Word List you can export as a spreadsheet or flashcards
Everything Translate does
Screenshot ⌥A

Capture it, mark it up, send it off

The screen freezes before you select, and the selection snaps to window edges. Ten annotation tools, and everything you draw stays movable and editable.

  • ↩ to copy, ⌘S to save, or pin it to the screen for reference
  • Recognize text, read QR codes and translate a screenshot in one step
  • Scrolling Capture: scroll inside the selection and it stitches everything into one image
  • Everything you capture is kept in Screenshot History, ready for more annotation
Everything Screenshot does

Small, fast and considerate

12 MB

Small and fast

Written natively, so the installer is only about a dozen megabytes. It lives in the menu bar and uses almost no CPU when idle.

⌥

Hands on the keyboard

Every feature has a shortcut. Panels don’t take focus, so what you pick pastes straight back into your app.

Local

Privacy stays on your Mac

Text recognition, language detection and sensitive-content detection all happen on your Mac, keys live in the system keychain, and no usage data is collected.

$0

Free

Free to download, with no ads; new versions install from inside the app in one click. The code is on GitHub.

And more

Not for every day, but right there when you need them.

Screen & Audio Recording

Record an area or a whole screen with system audio and the microphone, showing clicks and keys, then turn it into a GIF or compress it.

Notes

They wait on the edge of your screen and fan out when the pointer comes near; tasks, colors and images all fit, and they export as Markdown.

Launchpad

The familiar full-screen grid: page through it, search, make folders, and pinch the trackpad to open it. macOS 26 and later.

Status Screen

Cover every screen and block the keyboard and mouse in one press, to wipe the screen, let a job run or step away.

Dock Preview

Hover over a Dock icon to see thumbnails of all its windows, and click one to switch to it.

Notch

Copied, saved: notices grow out of the notch, recording timers, the song that’s playing and your Focus sit beside it, and a click brings up your quick actions.

There’s a Windows version too

Clipboard, Translate, Launcher, Screenshot, screen and audio recording, Notes, Reminders, Status Screen and Keep Awake, looking and working like the Mac version; snippets, favorites, the Word List, notes and reminders sync between the two. For Windows 11 (22H2 or later), x64 and ARM64. Its interface is in Chinese for now.
